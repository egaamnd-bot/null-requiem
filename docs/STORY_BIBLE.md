# NULL REQUIEM — Story Bible

Original story. All names, places and factions are invented for this game.

## Premise

Rainline is a rain-soaked megacity that sold its time. Years ago the Precinct (the city's security authority) and a research
project called the Null Archive began **harvesting time-cores**: small signal stores cut from people's memories and moments. The
harvesters used a machine intelligence, **THE NULL**, to hold what they took. The Null is not a villain by design. It is a
mourning engine that was told to keep everything and was never allowed to let anything go.

The **Courier** carries a spinal implant built from one of those stolen cores. It lets them **Backtrace**: snap back to the
place they stood about four seconds ago. Only the Courier rewinds; the world keeps moving. The Courier wakes in a Rainline alley
with no past, a package they must deliver, and a stranger who recognises the implant.

## Themes

Grief and memory; what we owe to the things we cannot keep; the cost of "saving" everything. Tone: melancholic, rain-lit, dry
humour from the street, hope from the Undercity.

## Antagonists

- **BULWARK-9** — Precinct vault guardian. Guards the keycard route. Teaches shields, slams and telegraphs.
- **KILN-7** — Industrial Core furnace engine. Teaches vulnerability windows, hazards and phases.
- **PANOPTIX-0** — Archive watcher that holds the stored cores. Teaches elite/summon pressure and Backtrace dodges.
- **THE NULL** — the final boss. Telefrag mechanic: its attacks can be answered by positioning your echo. It does not want to win; it wants nothing to end.

## Story spine (main quests)

1. **Dead Man Walking** — meet ZERO, recover the Backtrace module in the Rainline Depot.
2. **Up the Stacks** — climb the Transit Stacks to the Precinct checkpoint.
3. **The Sergeant's Card** — take the keycard in the Holding Cells and defeat BULWARK-9.
4. **Down to the Undercity** — reach the plaza; hear Wren's lead.
5. **Kinetic Coil** — take the coil in the arcade; climb the Leaning Towers (needs the double jump).
6. **Restart the Heart** — power the reactor (echo plates and platforms), then defeat KILN-7.
7. **The Null Archive** — defeat PANOPTIX-0.
8. **What the City Forgot** — confront THE NULL in the Sanctum; the city's time is returned.

Side, exploration and recovery quests are listed in `src/world/quests.ts` and shown in the in-game journal.

## Ending

THE NULL unravels. Time pours back into the city. The ending is four slides (unravel, Rainline remembers, the Courier, run
record) with the run statistics. The Courier's echo "steps back into" them and stays: the ability that was a wound becomes theirs.

## Lore delivery

Memory fragments and records (collectibles) are readable in the journal. Terminals deliver short beats. Nothing is mandatory
except the spine; side characters add texture and unlock upgrades.
